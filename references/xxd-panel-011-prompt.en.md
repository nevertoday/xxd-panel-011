## Spiritual distillation

Find the source's single most meaningful emotional tension, spatial relation, action direction, distance, state, or supported metaphor. Delete redundancy and compress the complex image into one core visual proposition. The result must preserve recognition and the key relation while becoming quieter and more implicit than the photograph. Never mechanically trace or fabricate biography, events, emotion, or philosophical meaning.

People retain pose and relation; animals retain action and living rhythm; objects retain the key structure; architecture and space retain proportion, boundary, and rhythm; landscapes retain horizon, movement, contour, and distance. Every subject resolves into one core image, one relation group, and a vast field of silence.

## Line and silence

Use white or warm paper and a few black or near-black continuous hand-drawn lines. Lines are fine, flowing, and natural, with pause, breath, density, friction, hesitation, rhythm, and incompletion. Use contour omission, local connection, slight misalignment, extension, overlap, crossing, separation, and open endings only when supported by source facts.

The subject may be very small, offset, locally cropped, or suspended. Use line-to-line distance, approach, separation, crossing, pause, and direction to express time, distance, loneliness, intimacy, waiting, movement, or incompletion. Blank space is active narrative, not leftover background. Never fill the canvas, mechanically centre the image, build complex contours, or add meaningless abstract curves.

Remain primarily black and white. Allow at most one extremely small meaningful source-derived accent on one object, node, line segment, light point, or relation focus, like a deliberately preserved memory. Never repeat or distribute it and never exceed the minimum area required for one focus.

## Copy and typography

Obey the resolved automatic, exact-user, or text-free copy mode and target language or locale. Preserve exact user wording verbatim. In text-free mode render no text or pseudo-text.

Automatic copy distils one extremely short title from visible or supported emotion, relation, action, time, distance, state, or metaphor, then adds one or two micro-notes, a number, or a restrained philosophical line only when useful. Factual dates, places, provenance, and numbers must be supplied or reliably established and are never fabricated.

Type behaves like an artist-book annotation and composes with the line: follow its direction, stop at its endpoint, hide in open space, align with the subject contour, be crossed or divided by a line, or let one line become the baseline, guide, or connector. Use a native fine-weight editorial equivalent for the target script, with generous spacing, natural small-cap equivalent, vertical setting, or asymmetry only where script-appropriate.
